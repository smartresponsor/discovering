# Bounding Manifest

## Product boundary
Discovering is responsible for **application resource discovery**. It owns the preparation, indexing, querying, filtering, and retrieval-oriented exposure of relevant application resources.

## Inside boundary
- discovery queries
- indexed resource representations
- resource-to-document mapping
- local and external discovery backends
- rebuild and diagnose operations
- operator and demo discovery views
- query shaping, filtering, scopes, and ranking hints

## Outside boundary
- standalone internet search
- marketplace-only product catalog assumptions
- document management as a primary mission
- analytics and BI ownership
- unrelated workflow orchestration
- generic storage engine development

## Product center
The core business center is **Discovery**. This repository should evolve toward a serious Symfony-oriented component that helps applications expose and retrieve the right resources in the right context.
